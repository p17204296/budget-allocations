# Event planning

Open **Planning → Events** to plan a birthday, anniversary, trip, or another one-off expense. **Monthly what-if** still holds your monthly income and spending scenarios.

Create an event, give it a total budget, and optionally add its date. Each item has an expected cost and amount paid, so hotel deposits and partial payments work. Expected cost is the full price, including any deposit. Update the expected cost when the final price is confirmed.

The summary shows your budget, total item costs, paid, still to pay, and budget remaining or extra budget needed. Going over budget does not prevent saving your plan.

## Funding

The funding target is the higher of your budget or your item costs. This preserves any room in your budget while allowing you to see the money required if your plans grow.

Choose how you record money for each event:

| Method | What to enter | Additional savings needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money still available | Money set aside and still unspent | Target − payments − available money, with a minimum of zero |
| Total funded so far | All money committed, including money already spent | Target − total funded, with a minimum of zero |

For example, £1,200 of planned costs, £200 paid, and £300 still available need another £700. Recording £500 as total funded produces the same result. Switching methods converts the amount for you; complete any invalid fields before switching.

Both amounts are maintained manually. When you record a purchase in the available-money method, update the available balance to reflect money spent. With total funded, only increase the amount when committing more money. Recording a payment never transfers money or updates an account balance.

## Dates and monthly savings

**Have money ready by** initially follows the event date. Set a separate deadline for advance bookings, or choose **Use event date** to reconnect it.

The monthly suggestion divides additional savings across a contribution today, then monthly on today's day of month through the deadline, inclusive. Month-end dates use the final valid day of each month. Suggestions round up to the currency's smallest unit. The contribution count is shown beside the amount.

An overdue deadline shows money needed now. Without a deadline, the funding gap is shown without a monthly suggestion.

## Saving

A [floating save prompt](saving-changes.md) appears after edits and stays available while you scroll. **Save now** saves immediately; the inline save button remains available too.

Valid changes autosave six seconds after your last edit. **Save changes** saves immediately. Blank amounts do not become zero. Amount paid cannot exceed cost, and total funded must cover payments already recorded.

Failed saves retain your draft and offer **Retry save**. Navigation waits for edits to save; fix incomplete fields or explicitly discard them before leaving. Browser close/refresh warnings protect unsaved edits where supported.

If another tab saves first, your draft remains visible and saving stops. **Reload saved version** asks before discarding your local changes. If the event was deleted elsewhere, explicitly discard local edits to return.

Plans keep their original currency, support up to 200 items, and remain independent of monthly allocations. You can save up to 20 events. Deleting an event also deletes its items. Guest events expire with the seven-day guest workspace.
