# Event planning

Open **Planning → Events** to plan a birthday, anniversary, trip, or another one-off expense. **Monthly what-if** still holds your monthly income and spending scenarios.

Create an event, give it a total budget, and optionally add its date. Each item has an expected cost and amount paid, so hotel deposits and partial payments work. Expected cost is the full price, including any deposit. Update the expected cost when the final price is confirmed.

**Add item** creates an item and takes you directly to its description. On mobile, a floating **+** button stays available as you scroll and performs the same action. It sits above the bottom navigation and moves above the save prompt when needed.

The summary shows your budget, total item costs, paid, still to pay, overpayments above item costs, and budget remaining or extra budget needed. Each item’s unpaid amount and overpayment are calculated separately, so an overpayment on one item does not offset another item’s unpaid amount. Going over budget does not prevent saving your plan.

## Your savings plan

The funding target is the higher of your budget or your item costs. This preserves any room in your budget while allowing you to see the money required if your plans grow.

**Already funded before this plan** is money set aside for the event before the savings schedule starts. You can start with zero, a small amount, or the entire target. Don’t include savings you enter in the monthly rows in this starting amount as well. Payments are tracked separately by default.

For example, with a £1,200 target and £300 set aside, £900 remains in the savings plan whether you have paid £200 toward the event or not. A payment updates the event’s paid and remaining-to-pay figures, separately from savings progress. Payments may exceed an item’s expected cost; the overage is shown and does not prevent saving.

Turn on **Track payments against these savings** if event payments should come from the savings recorded in this plan. The app then shows a non-blocking warning when recorded payments exceed starting funding plus actual savings. Leave it off when payments can come from other sources.

**Actually saved** records additional money set aside during each month. The starting funding plus all actual monthly savings is your total funding recorded. The amount still needed is the target minus that total, with a minimum of zero. When funding reaches or exceeds the target, the savings plan is **Fully funded**. This tracks savings progress; payments are tracked separately and do not change the savings amount still needed.

All figures are maintained manually. Recording funding, savings or payments does not transfer money or update account balances. These plans remain separate from monthly allocations.

Existing events automatically convert their old funding method when opened: unspent available money plus payments becomes starting funding; total funded stays the same. Their funding gap is preserved. The converted plan is stored on the next save.

## Dates and monthly savings

**Start saving from** initially uses today. For an existing event whose deadline has passed, it uses the deadline so the historical plan remains editable. Once saved, the start date stays fixed until you change it.

**Have money ready by** initially follows the event date. Set a separate deadline for advance bookings, or choose **Use event date** to reconnect it. The start date must be on or before the deadline. Without a deadline, you can save the event and starting amount, then add a monthly schedule later.

The schedule includes both the start and deadline months, with full names and years. January 31 through March 1 gives January, February and March; the final contribution must be available by March 1. The initial suggestion divides the amount left after starting funding across the months, distributing any pennies so the total is exact. A fully funded event starts with zero planned savings.

Edit **Planned savings** for each month to fit your circumstances, and update **Actually saved** as you go. Actual savings may exceed the plan. Planned amounts remain fixed when you record savings or change the budget, so you can compare your original intentions with your progress.

Every month between your chosen start and deadline counts, including past months. The app does not silently drop October when November begins. For example, a £2,940 target from October through January with no starting funding or recorded savings rebalances to **£735 in each of the four months**.

When expected funding differs from the target, the rebalance button is highlighted and a message explains the shortfall or optional reduction. Choose **Keep my plan** to decline without changing your figures. The same suggestion stays dismissed in that browser tab, including after a reload. A different shortfall, surplus or date range can prompt again. Suggestions are hidden while inputs are incomplete.

**Rebalance savings plan** spreads the amount still needed across all selected months with zero actual savings, including past months. Months with recorded savings retain both their original target and actual amount. Their actual savings reduce what must be planned for the other months. The action also works for historical schedules. If a month was skipped, set its planned amount to zero; change the start date to exclude it from subsequent rebalances.

Changing dates preserves overlapping entries and adds new months with zero amounts. Months excluded by the new dates appear under **Outside current schedule**. Their actual savings still count toward funding; their planned amounts are excluded from the active totals. Use the remove button to delete an outside month and both of its amounts; this cannot be undone. If the schedule later includes that month again, it is added with zero amounts. Rebalance when you want suggestions for the revised dates.

**Planned funding** is starting funding plus the sum of every planned amount in the selected schedule. It matches the monthly planned entries even when actual savings differ.

**Expected funding** is starting funding plus recorded actual savings, plus planned amounts for selected months with zero actual savings. A nonzero actual amount replaces that month's plan in this total. Actual savings outside the schedule still count. Zero actual savings means the plan is still included, regardless of the calendar date. This is an expectation, not money already funded.

For example, £735 planned in each of four months totals £2,940. Recording £500 actual savings in October leaves the planned total at £2,940 but changes expected funding to £2,705: £500 actual plus three £735 plans. Rebalance retains October's original £735 target and divides the remaining £2,440 across the other three months (£813.34, £813.33, £813.33).

Existing monthly plans require no data migration. They open with their saved amounts intact, show the correct totals, and can be corrected with **Rebalance savings plan**. Legacy events without monthly savings still convert their aggregate funding as described above.

Schedules support up to 1,200 retained monthly entries, including entries outside the current date range.

## Event notes

Use **Event notes** for booking details, ideas and reminders. Notes are optional plain text, support up to 5,000 characters and save with the rest of the event.

## Saving

A [floating save prompt](saving-changes.md) appears after edits and stays available while you scroll. **Save now** saves immediately; the inline save button remains available too.

Valid changes autosave six seconds after your last edit. **Save changes** saves immediately. Blank amounts do not become zero. Payments may exceed expected costs; overages are shown in the item and event summary. Complete any invalid inputs before saving.

Failed saves retain your draft and offer **Retry save**. Navigation waits for edits to save; fix incomplete fields or explicitly discard them before leaving. Browser close/refresh warnings protect unsaved edits where supported.

If another tab saves first, your draft remains visible and saving stops. **Reload saved version** asks before discarding your local changes. If the event was deleted elsewhere, explicitly discard local edits to return.

Plans keep their original currency, support up to 200 items and remain independent of monthly allocations. You can save up to 20 events. Deleting an event also deletes its items, savings plan and notes. Guest events expire with the seven-day guest workspace.
